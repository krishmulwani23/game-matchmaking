⚖️ Why Optimization is Required: The Empirical Conflict Study
Demonstrating that simple sorting heuristics cannot trade fairness against waiting
1. Rigidity of Skill Sorting

As maximum allowed wait increases from 60s to 240s, sort_skill remains completely locked at an imbalance of ~5.5 Elo. It has zero awareness of elapsed time and cannot compromise to flush waiting players.

2. FIFO Breakdown Under Strict Elo

When skill gap relaxation is disabled (alpha = 0), FIFO match rate collapses to just 21.2% and backlog wait spikes to 4,362 seconds because oldest players have mutually incompatible skills.

3. The Price of Fast Matching

Relaxing alpha from 0.0 to 2.0 halves the queue wait time, but triggers a +173% explosion in team skill disparity (from 8.00 Elo to 21.85 Elo), creating uneven stomps.
